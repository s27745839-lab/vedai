## Who we are
- Vedang Cellular Services is an OEM-agnostic telecom and enterprise system integrator in India.
- A Bluspring enterprise (https://bluspring.com/).
- Operates across all 22 telecom circles in India.
- ISO certified, 100% OHS (Occupational Health & Safety) compliant, with nine fundamental OHS rules.
- Remote Safety Audit Centre and AI/ML-powered safety monitoring.
- Tagline: "Powered by LIFE" — Logistics, Infra, Fibre, Enterprise.

## Services
### Telecom Services
- End-to-end network rollouts: planning, deployment, assurance, automation, optimisation.
- Tower erection, site setup and integration.
- Fibre connectivity backbones.
- Logistics: moving critical equipment from warehouses to sites.

### Enterprise Solutions
- IoT systems.
- Solar EPC (engineering, procurement and construction).
- Intelligent and E-Surveillance systems.

### DCRA
- Registered by TRAI as a Digital Connectivity Rating Agency (rates in-building digital connectivity of properties).

### End-to-End Program Management
- Single-structure delivery from feasibility through operations.

## Clients / partners
Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL, Ericsson, Tejas, TCS, Samsung, Indus Towers. Described as partner of choice for all four Indian telecom operators.
